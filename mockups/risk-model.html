Application risk analytics · the data model

Pipeline built with an AI coding agent from APIs and manual files, shaped in Python, presented in Power BI.

  • scan_id

Remediation
  • finding_id
  • closed_at
  • fix_type
  • days_open
SLA policy
  • severity
  • window_days
  • effective_from

coverage = repos with a scan in the window / repos in scope, by app
within SLA = closed_at − opened_at ≤ window_days for that severity
mean time to resolve = average days_open of findings closed that week

The decisions this supports

Gives business leaders a way to ask where exposure is known, where visibility is incomplete, and whether remediation is keeping pace. The same questions guide the move from clearing a backlog to ongoing risk monitoring.

1 · Do we know all our risks?

In scope, scanned, findings by severity and SLA.

Application → Repository → Scan → Finding
2 · Are we fixing them in time?

Burn-down against SLA, worst severity first.

Finding → Remediation, SLA policy
3 · When are we done?

Mean time to resolve, held inside the window.

Remediation, SLA policy, by week

Done is a steady state. New findings close within SLA as fast as they arrive, and the mean resolution time stays inside the window. That is the measure once it is business as usual.

Fictional SLA windows: critical 2 weeks, high 6 weeks, medium 12 weeks, low next release. Every count synthetic.
